## Why Irradiance Matters

The red light therapy market is flooded with panels claiming impressive wattages, LED counts, and coverage areas. Many of these numbers say little about output. The specification most relevant to dose is **irradiance** — the amount of light energy hitting your skin per unit area, measured in milliwatts per square centimetre (mW/cm²).

The therapeutic dose for red light therapy is expressed as **energy density** (joules per cm²), calculated as:

**Irradiance (mW/cm²) × Time (seconds) = Energy Density (J/cm²)**

Target therapeutic doses for most applications fall between 10 and 40 J/cm². A panel delivering 50 mW/cm² needs 200–800 seconds (3–13 minutes) to reach that dose. A panel delivering 100 mW/cm² needs half the time — 100–400 seconds (1.5–7 minutes).

A panel that delivers a lower irradiance at treatment distance needs proportionally longer sessions to reach the same dose.

## Dual-Chip Technology: Why It Matters

The MitoPRO 1500 uses dual-chip LEDs — each physical LED houses both a 660nm (red) emitter and an 850nm (near-infrared) emitter in a single chip package.

Competing panels often use alternating rows of red and NIR LEDs. While functional, this creates subtle striping in the irradiance pattern — areas slightly richer in red or NIR depending on your distance and position.

According to the manufacturer, dual-chip LEDs mix both wavelengths at every point in the treatment area. For most users this is likely a minor point, but it may matter for precise facial or targeted treatment.

### Wavelength Accuracy

Mito Red Light states 660nm and 850nm wavelengths for this panel. We have not verified them. Wavelength accuracy matters because photobiomodulation research generally uses red and near-infrared wavelengths in specific ranges, and a panel whose output is well outside those ranges may not match the studies. Ask for the manufacturer's spectral test report if this matters to you.

## How to Use the MitoPRO 1500: Protocol Guide

### Treatment Distance
- **6 inches:** Maximum irradiance — appropriate for body, joint, and muscle treatments
- **12 inches:** Moderate irradiance — appropriate for face (more comfortable, reduced heat sensation)
- **18 inches:** Lower irradiance — appropriate for children or sensitive skin

### Duration at 6 Inches
- **5–10 minutes per area** delivers 29–58 J/cm² — within the therapeutic window for most applications
- Longer is not better: a biphasic dose-response exists. Beyond 60 J/cm², cellular benefits plateau or reverse. Do not exceed 15 minutes per area.

### Frequency
- **3–5 sessions per week** for therapeutic goals (muscle recovery, skin, joint pain)
- Daily use is safe but benefits plateau without rest days for some applications
- A single dose 2–4 hours post-exercise shows the best muscle recovery data

### Timing
- **Morning:** For energy, cognitive benefits, circadian alignment
- **Post-workout:** For muscle recovery and inflammation reduction
- **Avoid within 2 hours of sleep:** High-intensity red light can suppress melatonin in some individuals

### Eye Safety
- Never look directly at the LEDs
- Closing eyes during facial treatment is adequate for most people
- Goggles are optional but recommended for those who are photosensitive
