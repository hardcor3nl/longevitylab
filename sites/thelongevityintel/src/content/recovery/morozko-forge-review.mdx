**The short answer:** The Morozko Forge is priced at $12,490 for the 60 in tub ($13,990 for the 66 in tub) on Morozko's own page (checked 29 Sept 2026), not the $7,990 an earlier version of this page gave. Morozko lists a 32 F minimum with ice-making, chlorine-free microfiltration plus ozone, 79 or 85 inch lengths, a standard 110 V cord with inline GFCI and a 1-year parts-and-labor warranty. Buy it only if you specifically want ice-cold water, quiet operation and a premium build. The research does not show that colder water gives more benefit, and cold immersion has no proven longevity effect.

## What the research says

- **Recovery.** A [2023 meta-analysis of 28 studies](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01800-1) found cold water immersion better than other recovery methods for muscle soreness. The abstract does not say colder water worked better; temperature and time were rarely moderators.
- **Strength training.** A [2026 network meta-analysis of 87 studies](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13102-026-01653-5) found passive recovery did best for strength gains, moderate certainty; its ranges for cold protocols were 5 to 8 C, 9 to 12 C and 13 to 20 C, all above freezing.
- **Cold adaptation.** A [2021 study of winter-swimming men](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2021.100408) found they had greater cold-induced thermogenesis than controls; it did not test tub temperatures or health outcomes.
- **Safety.** A [physiology review](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2012.229864) reports cold water can trigger heart-rhythm problems in healthy volunteers.

We found no study showing that near-freezing water beats cooler-but-not-freezing water for any health outcome.

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Pick it if** you already plunge regularly, want ice-cold water, quiet running and a compact premium build, and the price is not the deciding factor.

**Skip it if** this is your first cold plunge (a cheaper tub or a cold shower tests the habit; see [cold showers](/protocols/cold-shower-protocol)), you have any heart, blood pressure or circulation condition (ask your doctor), or you lift for strength and want the evidence above.
