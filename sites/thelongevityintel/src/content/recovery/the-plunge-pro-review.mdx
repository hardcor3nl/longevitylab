**The short answer:** Plunge's site does not list a product called "Plunge Pro" ("Pro Chiller" is the name of its faster chiller). Its main home cold plunges are The Plunge (regular $8,490, $6,790 on sale), the All-In Gen 2 (regular $9,990, $7,990 on sale) and the Pod (regular $6,990, $5,590 on sale), checked 29 Sept 2026 during a Labor Day sale; cheaper Plunge Air ($5,490) and Plunge Pop-Up ($4,490) tubs also exist and are not covered here. Plunge lists cooling to 37 F with its Pro Chiller, a 1-year warranty and a dedicated 120 V, 15 A circuit. Pick the All-In if you want the chiller built in; pick the Pod if space is tight. Evidence supports cold water for muscle soreness, not longevity, and it can be risky for people with heart conditions.

## What the research says

- **Recovery.** A [2023 meta-analysis of 28 studies](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01800-1) found cold water immersion better than other recovery methods for muscle soreness after hard exercise and similar for power and flexibility.
- **Strength training.** A [2026 network meta-analysis of 87 studies](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13102-026-01653-5) found passive recovery did best for strength gains in resistance training, with certainty rated moderate; cold immersion after lifting may not help adaptation. Evidence is low certainty and 85% of participants were male.
- **Safety.** A [physiology review](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2012.229864) notes cold water can trigger cardiac arrhythmias in healthy volunteers through simultaneous cold shock and diving responses.
- **Longevity.** We found no trial showing cold plunging extends life or improves general health. See [cold plunge evidence](/recovery/cold-plunge-ice-bath-review).

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Pick a Plunge if** you already like cold immersion, have the outdoor or garage space and circuit, and accept the 1,000-plus lb floor load when full. A chest freezer or a cheaper unit is a fair way to test whether you will keep using it.

**Skip it if** you have any heart, blood pressure or circulation condition (ask your doctor first), you mainly want strength gains (see the meta-analysis above), or you want a proven longevity tool. Protocols: [cold exposure](/protocols/cold-exposure-protocol) and [cold showers](/protocols/cold-shower-protocol).
