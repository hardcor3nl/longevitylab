**The short answer:** Massage has real but modest evidence. A 2026 meta-analysis of 88 randomised trials (5,524 adults) of manual therapy, mainly massage, found less pain (VAS down 16 points), systolic blood pressure lower by 3.91 mmHg, better Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index scores (down 4.06) and lower anxiety, and called the evidence preliminary. Earlier meta-analyses found massage after hard exercise lowers soreness. None shows longer life. If you like it, it is a reasonable low-risk habit; if you want a cheap start, a $39.99 foam roller has a meta-analysis behind it for range of motion.

## What the trials show

| Outcome | Result | Source | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | VAS down 16.01 points vs controls | [Manual therapy meta-analysis, 88 trials, 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1815296) | Preliminary; mixed conditions |
| Blood pressure | Systolic down 3.91 mmHg, heart rate down 4.2 beats a minute | Same | Short-term; do not stop BP treatment |
| Sleep and anxiety | PSQI down 4.06; STAI anxiety down 9.68 | Same | Small trials, self-reported |
| Soreness after exercise | Lower at 24, 48 and 72 hours (overall SMD -1.16) | [11 trials, 504 people, 2017](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2017.00747) | Mostly manual massage |
| Range of motion | Foam rolling d = 0.76 (13 studies) | [32-study review, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbmt.2020.01.007) | Benefit beyond range of motion not shown |
| Massage gun | 40 minutes lowered soreness vs stretching at 48 h (30 men) | [RCT, 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1561970) | One small trial |

We could not verify older often-quoted figures (for example a 30% cortisol drop from one session), so we do not use them.

## Which option for which goal

- **You want relief from soreness or tightness:** foam roller first ($39.99 GRID 1.0 from TriggerPoint; Therabody lists Theragun Prime at $329.99 and PRO at $529.99). Compare guns in [best massage guns](/recovery/best-massage-guns-percussive-therapy).
- **You want help with stress, sleep or blood pressure:** a professional session is what the trials tested, but effects are small; it is a comfort measure, not a treatment.
- **You want a recovery stack:** massage is one option among [compression](/recovery/compression-therapy-guide), [cold immersion](/recovery/cold-plunge-ice-bath-review) and sleep; see [best recovery devices](/best/best-recovery-devices-2024).

We do not quote a frequency or a session price: the trials vary and prices differ by city.

## Who should skip it or ask first

Ask a doctor before massage if you have a blood clot or clotting disorder, take anticoagulants, have cancer, fractures, skin infections, severe osteoporosis or unexplained swelling or pain. Do not use massage to replace treatment for high blood pressure or persistent pain.
