**The short answer:** compression garments give small recovery benefits, and pneumatic boots such as Normatec show weaker evidence. A 2017 meta-analysis found an overall effect size of 0.38 for garments, largest for strength recovery and after resistance exercise. For boots, a 2024 meta-analysis of 17 studies (319 people) found trivial to small effects on muscle function and trivial to moderate effects on soreness; a 2025 meta-analysis found no reduction in soreness at 24 hours (moderate certainty). Normatec 3 legs list at $899 and the full-body system at $1,549 on Hyperice's site (29 Sept 2026). Compression is not a longevity intervention. Talk to your doctor before using compression if you have circulation problems or clots.

## What the evidence shows

Grade: Emerging to Moderate for garments; Emerging to Low for boots.

| Question | Evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Garments, recovery from exercise | [Meta-analysis 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-017-0728-9): overall ES 0.38 (CI 0.25 to 0.51); strength recovery ES 0.62; largest benefits after resistance exercise | Effects small; pressure and training-status effects unclear |
| Garments, muscle damage | [Meta-analysis 2014](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2013-092456), 12 studies: DOMS g = 0.40, strength 0.46, power 0.49, creatine kinase 0.44, all moderate | Older and measured at 24 to 72 hours |
| Boots (pneumatic), sports recovery | [17 studies, 319 people, 2024](https://doi.org/10.5114/biolsport.2024.133665): trivial to small benefit for muscle function, trivial to moderate for soreness, highly variable for muscle damage markers; protocols often 20 to 30 minutes at about 80 mmHg | Small studies, fair to good quality |
| Boots, soreness at 24 hours | [Meta-analysis 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40954632/): no reduction (MD -2.20 points, CI -10.08 to 5.68), moderate certainty | Single study behind that estimate |
| Boots, performance | [21 trained cyclists](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28872381/): no significant difference in a 4-minute time trial after 30 minutes of boots vs rest (d = -0.08) | One protocol, trained athletes |

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Reasonable to try if** you train hard and already like a compression garment or boots for the feel. Garments are far cheaper and have the better evidence; boots are a comfort tool.

**Skip it, and ask a doctor first, if** you have circulation problems, blood clots, heart failure, skin infections or unexplained leg swelling: compression may be unsuitable in these situations.
