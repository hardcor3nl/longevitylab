**The short answer:** compression garments give small recovery benefits. A 2017 meta-analysis found an overall effect size of 0.38, largest for strength recovery (2 to 8 hours and beyond 24 hours) and after resistance exercise; the benefit in relation to pressure and training status was unclear. We did not verify evidence for pneumatic compression boots, so this page makes no claim about them, and compression is not a longevity intervention. Talk to your doctor before using compression if you have circulation problems or clots.
