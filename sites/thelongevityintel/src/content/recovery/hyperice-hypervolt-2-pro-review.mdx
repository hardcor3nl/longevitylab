**The short answer:** Hyperice's current premium gun is the Hypervolt 3 Pro, listed at $349 with a stated 51 dB noise level, up to 4 hours of battery, six speeds (1500 to 2500 RPM), a pressure sensor and a heated head (Hyperice, checked 29 Sept 2026). The Hypervolt 2 Pro this page covers cost $330 in a December 2022 iRunFar review, which reported a 60 to 70 lb stall force, about 2.6 lb weight and up to 3 hours of battery, and Hyperice's product URL for it now shows the 3 Pro. Buy it if quiet operation matters to you; nothing in the research says it works better than a cheaper gun.

A Forbes review reported 35 lb of stall force for the 2 Pro, against Hyperice's own 60 to 70 lb, so treat stall force as unsettled. Earlier versions of this page listed 12 mm, 40 lb, 1.8 lb, 55 dB and 180 minutes; we could not confirm any of them and removed them.

## What the research says

Percussive massage has small trials, not proof. A [2025 trial of 30 active men](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1561970) found 40 minutes of percussive massage left lower soreness and better jump measures at 48 hours than static stretching after a soreness-inducing squat protocol. A [2026 trial of 37 men](https://doi.org/10.1002/pri.70246) found no significant strength benefit over rest and called the effects preliminary. Neither compared brands. More in [best massage guns](/recovery/best-massage-guns-percussive-therapy).

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Pick a Hypervolt if** noise is your deciding factor (shared home, early mornings) and you will use the app's pressure guidance. Compare the deeper-stroke option in the [Theragun PRO review](/recovery/theragun-pro-gen6-review), which Therabody lists at $529.99.

**Skip it if** you only want to try percussive massage: a cheaper gun tests that just as well (see the roundup). Skip and ask a clinician first if you take anticoagulants, have a clotting disorder, or have pain or swelling that is not normal post-exercise soreness. Other options: [compression therapy](/recovery/compression-therapy-guide), [massage and bodywork](/recovery/massage-bodywork-recovery).
