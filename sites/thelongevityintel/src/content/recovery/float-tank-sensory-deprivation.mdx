**The short answer:** the float tank research points to benefits for stress, anxiety and pain, and no clear benefit for sleep. A 2025 systematic review of 63 studies (1,838 participants) found generally positive effects on pain, athletic performance, stress, mental well-being and clinical anxiety, and limited to no effect on sleep-related disorders. It included studies of all designs, including preprints, so it does not show how strong each finding is. Nothing here supports a longevity claim. Talk to your doctor if you have a medical or psychiatric condition.
