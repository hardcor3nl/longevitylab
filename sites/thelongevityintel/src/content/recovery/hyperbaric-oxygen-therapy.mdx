**The short answer:** hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) is a medical treatment with FDA-cleared uses, not a proven longevity therapy. The FDA's current consumer page, "Get the Facts", lists 13 conditions for which chambers are cleared (as of July 2021), including carbon monoxide poisoning, decompression sickness, severe burns and diabetic foot ulcers, and says HBOT devices are not proven to cure cancer, Lyme disease, autism or Alzheimer's disease. We found no trial supporting anti-aging claims. Use it under a doctor's care, at a properly accredited facility, for cleared conditions.

## What the FDA says now

Read on 29 Sept 2026 from the Internet Archive copy (18 Dec 2025) of the FDA page, because the live address returned "not found" to our reader. The page states "Content current as of 07/26/2021".

| Point | FDA wording (paraphrased) |
|---|---|
| Cleared conditions | Air and gas bubbles in blood vessels; severe anemia when transfusion cannot be used; severe burns; carbon monoxide poisoning; crush injury; decompression sickness; gas gangrene; sudden complete hearing loss of unknown cause; severe skin and bone infection; radiation injury; skin graft flap at risk; sudden painless vision loss in one eye from blocked blood flow; non-healing wounds including diabetic foot ulcers |
| Not proven | Cancer, Lyme disease, autism or Alzheimer's disease |
| COVID-19 and others | No HBOT device cleared or authorized for COVID-19 or any condition beyond the list |
| Risks | Ear and sinus pain, middle ear injury, temporary vision changes, rare lung collapse; oxygen at high concentration is a fire risk |
| Where | Under a provider's advice, at a hospital or facility inspected and accredited by the Undersea and Hyperbaric Medical Society |
| Home devices | The FDA cleared a zippered bag for altitude sickness only, not for use with oxygen; it is aware of people using these to make homemade HBOT devices, with fire and suffocation risk |

The FDA also sent a [letter to providers on 25 Aug 2025](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/letters-health-care-providers/follow-instructions-safe-use-hyperbaric-oxygen-therapy-devices-letter-health-care-providers) about "serious injuries and deaths" from recent HBOT device fires, saying serious events are rare but "can and do occur".

What changed from the wording this page used before: the earlier page quoted a 2013 version that said HBOT was not proven for cancer, autism or diabetes and cited 27 complaints; the current page names cancer, Lyme disease, autism and Alzheimer's, lists diabetic foot ulcers as cleared, and drops the complaint count.

## Where longevity claims stand

Grade: Not shown. We did not find a randomised trial of HBOT for healthy ageing, and the FDA page lists no anti-aging use. Home "mild" chambers are a separate product class this page does not evaluate.

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Reasonable to consider (with a doctor) if** you have one of the cleared conditions and your provider recommends it at an accredited facility.

**Skip it if** you want it as a general longevity or wellness treatment, and never build a chamber from a bag and an oxygen source. Ask your doctor first if you have lung disease or ear or sinus problems.
