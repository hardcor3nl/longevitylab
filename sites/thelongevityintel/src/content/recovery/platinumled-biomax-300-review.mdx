**The short answer:** PlatinumLED's site lists the BIOMAX 300, 450, 600 and 900 as its 9th-generation series (29 Sept 2026), with the 600 at $1,049 and the 900 at $1,299, and a 3-year warranty on all products. We found no independent measurement of the 300, and the figures the old version of this page gave for it (price, "excellent irradiance") could not be traced, so they are removed. The nearest independent data is a spectrometer review of the larger 900. See the [three-way comparison](/recovery/joovv-vs-mito-red-vs-platinumled) and the [evidence by outcome](/recovery/red-light-therapy-evidence).

The reviewer discloses being affiliated with PlatinumLED (5% commission through links). It is one reviewer, one unit and an earlier generation, so treat the figure as indicative. A smaller panel like the 300 will have lower total power; do not read the 900's numbers as the 300's.
