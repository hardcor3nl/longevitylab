**The short answer:** PlatinumLED sells the BIOMAX 300 at $659, 450 at $799, 600 at $1,049 and 900 at $1,299, plus a newer BIOMAX PRO line (NANO $799, MIDI $999, GRANDE $1,299, ULTRA $1,599) and a $299 portable BIOMAX GO, all with a 3-year warranty and a 60-day trial, per the brand (checked 29 Sept 2026). We found no independent measurement of the 300; the nearest independent data is a spectrometer review of the 900. The figures earlier versions of this page gave for the 300 could not be traced and were removed. No trial shows that a panel with more output gives better results: see the [evidence by outcome](/recovery/red-light-therapy-evidence).

## What is independently measured

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Brand irradiance method | Handheld solar-meter centre-point test, and a third-party spectrometer test sampled at one-inch intervals in a small area; numbers are in chart images | [PlatinumLED](https://platinumtherapylights.com/pages/how-to-read-joules-vs-mw) |
| BioMax 900 (8th generation, Jul 2024) | Peak 113, average 90.3 mW/cm2 at 6 inches; 75 at 12 inches; 186 W; seven wavelengths from 480 to 1,060 nm | [Light Therapy Insiders](https://www.lighttherapyinsiders.com/platinumled-biomax-900-review/) |
| Reviewer's price and terms then | $1,299, later about $1,339; 3-year warranty; 60-day return with a 20% restocking fee | Same |

The reviewer discloses being affiliated with PlatinumLED (5% commission through links). It is one reviewer, one unit and an earlier generation, so treat the figure as indicative. A smaller panel like the 300 has lower total power; do not read the 900's numbers as the 300's.

## Which one, if any

- **You want the only independently measured model:** the 900, at $1,299 (the reviewer's average of 90.3 mW/cm2 at 6 inches works out at about $14.4 per mW/cm2 by our arithmetic).
- **You want to try red light cheaply:** the 300 at $659 or the GO at $299 are the lowest entry points; whether they deliver useful dose is unmeasured.
- **You want to compare brands:** see the [three-way comparison](/recovery/joovv-vs-mito-red-vs-platinumled), the [Joovv Solo 3.0](/recovery/joovv-solo-3-review) and [Mito Red MitoPRO 1500](/recovery/mito-red-light-mitopro-1500-review) reviews, and the [panel roundup](/recovery/best-red-light-therapy-panels-2024).

**Skip it if** you expect proven anti-aging or fat-loss effects (the evidence is mixed by outcome), you have a light-sensitive condition or take photosensitising medicines (ask your doctor), or you cannot use the 60-day trial to decide. PlatinumLED panels are also on [Amazon](/go/biomax-300) (we may earn a commission; the link is a search).
