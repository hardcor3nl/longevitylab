**The short answer:** The mPulse Believe is Sunlighten's two-person "intelligent" sauna. Sunlighten says its PulseIQ platform controls red light, near, mid and far infrared separately, with six programs (detox, weight loss, heart health, relaxation, muscle recovery, anti-aging) that it says are built from 37 clinical studies. There is no online price: mPulse is quoted by consultant. Sunlighten's simpler lines list from $2,099 (Solo), $5,099 (Signature) and $5,799 (Amplify). If you want a home infrared sauna, ask what each program actually delivers; the trials on infrared sauna are small and none shows longer life.

## What the research says

- **Heart failure.** A [2018 meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1002/clc.23077) of nine studies found that infrared sauna sessions of the kind used in those studies (15 minutes at 60 C, then a 30-minute rest, five times a week for 2 to 4 weeks) were linked to lower BNP and better ejection fraction in heart-failure patients, with no effect on blood pressure. Moderate to insufficient evidence.
- **Healthy women.** A [2022 crossover trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ctim.2021.102798) of 10 women found an infrared sauna raised body temperature but did not change blood pressure, arterial stiffness or HRV differently from rest.
- **Programs.** We could not find which 37 studies Sunlighten's six programs draw on, so we cannot say whether a given program (for example "weight loss") is backed by trials.

The wider longevity evidence is about traditional saunas; see [home saunas: what the studies show](/recovery/best-home-saunas-infrared-2024).

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Pick it if** you want separate control of infrared bands, have budget for a consultant-quoted premium unit, and see it as a comfort and routine purchase.

**Skip it if** you want a proven health intervention, a fixed online price, or the lowest cost: a Solo ($2,099) or Signature ($5,099) costs less than either premium cabin; see also the [Clearlight Sanctuary 2](/recovery/clearlight-sanctuary-2-review), which retailers list at about $7,300 to $7,800. Ask your doctor first if you are pregnant, have heart disease or low blood pressure, or take medicines that affect heat tolerance. Routine details: [sauna protocol](/protocols/sauna-protocol).
