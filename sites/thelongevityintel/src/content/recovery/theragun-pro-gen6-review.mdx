**The short answer:** Therabody's store lists the Theragun PRO as a 5th-generation device at $529.99 (120 minutes of battery, 2.2 lb, six attachments, OLED screen, one-year warranty), with the PRO Plus at $649.99. We found no "Pro Gen 6": the 6th Gen label on Therabody's store belongs to the Theragun Prime at $329.99. Therabody's buying guide lists 16 mm amplitude for the PRO, PRO Plus, Prime Plus and Prime. If you want the deepest stroke Therabody sells, that is the line; if you want to test whether percussive massage helps you, a cheaper gun is a fair trial. All checked 29 Sept 2026; Therabody's store data listed the PRO, PRO Plus and Prime as available that day.

## What the research says

- A [2017 meta-analysis of 11 trials](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2017.00747) (504 people, mostly manual massage) found massage after hard exercise lowered soreness at 24, 48 and 72 hours and modestly improved strength. It did not test massage guns.
- A [2025 trial of 30 active men](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1561970): 40 minutes of percussive massage produced lower soreness and better jump measures at 48 hours than static stretching.
- A [2026 trial of 37 men](https://doi.org/10.1002/pri.70246): no significant strength difference from rest; authors call the benefit preliminary.

Small trials, young men, short follow-up, no comparison of brands or amplitudes. We found no evidence that a longer stroke gives better recovery.

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Pick a Theragun if** you already know you use a massage gun regularly, want the app and screen, and would rather pay for the brand's deeper stroke and build. Wider comparison: [best massage guns](/recovery/best-massage-guns-percussive-therapy).

**Skip it if** you are trying percussive massage for the first time, want a quiet device (Therabody gives no dB figure; Hyperice lists 51 dB for the Hypervolt 3 Pro), or take anticoagulants, have a clotting disorder or have pain and swelling beyond normal soreness: ask a clinician first. Alternatives: [compression therapy](/recovery/compression-therapy-guide), [massage and bodywork](/recovery/massage-bodywork-recovery).
