**The short answer:** Clearlight's Sanctuary 2 is a two-person, full-spectrum infrared cabin: two 500 W True Wave heaters on the front wall plus far-infrared heaters on the back, sides, calves and under the floor, drawing 2,260 W on a 120 V, 20 A outlet (Clearlight's page, checked 29 Sept 2026). Clearlight calls it low-EMF and offers a residential lifetime warranty. Clearlight's page shows no price ("Get Pricing", plus a $500 sale banner and 18-month financing with $999 down), so the price comes from resellers: about $7,300 to $7,800 at three US retailers ([Evergreen Softub](https://evergreensoftub.com/products/clearlight-sanctuary-2-full-spectrum-two-person-infrared-sauna), [Purely Relaxation](https://www.purelyrelaxation.com/products/clearlight-sanctuary-2-full-spectrum-two-person-infrared-sauna), [Synergy Health](https://synergyhealthlive.com/products/clearlight%C2%AE-sanctuary-2-full-spectrum-two-person-infrared-sauna-with-chromotherapy)), depending on wood, with basswood the cheaper. An earlier version of this page said about $4,995; that was wrong. Buy it if you want a plug-in home cabin; do not buy it expecting proven life extension, because the trials do not show that.

## What the research says about infrared saunas

- **Heart failure.** A [2018 meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1002/clc.23077) of nine studies (infrared only) found sessions of 15 minutes at 60 C, five times a week for 2 to 4 weeks, were linked to lower BNP and better ejection fraction, with no effect on blood pressure. Patients, short-term, evidence rated moderate to insufficient.
- **Healthy women.** A [2022 crossover trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ctim.2021.102798) of 10 women found an infrared sauna raised body temperature but produced no significant differences in blood pressure, arterial stiffness or HRV against exercise or rest.
- **Longevity.** The big longevity numbers come from a [Finnish cohort](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2014.8187) of 2,315 men using traditional saunas; it is observational and not about infrared units. More in [home saunas: what the studies show](/recovery/best-home-saunas-infrared-2024).

On EMF, we found no independent measurement of this model; treat "low EMF" as a marketing claim.

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Pick it if** you have the floor space, a dedicated 20 A circuit and want a plug-in two-person cabin with a long warranty, and you understand the health claims are unproven. Sauna routines: [sauna protocol](/protocols/sauna-protocol).

**Skip it if** you are pregnant, have heart disease, low blood pressure or take medicines that affect heat tolerance (ask your doctor first), or you want a proven longevity intervention. Cheaper heat options exist; see the [cold plunge evidence](/recovery/cold-plunge-ice-bath-review) and other [recovery devices](/best/best-recovery-devices-2024).
