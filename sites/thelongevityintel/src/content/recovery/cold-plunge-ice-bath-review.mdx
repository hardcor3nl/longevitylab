**The short answer:** cold water immersion helps with muscle soreness after hard exercise. A 2023 meta-analysis of 28 studies in physically active people found it better than other common recovery methods for soreness and similar for recovery of power and flexibility. It says nothing about longevity or general health. Cold water stresses the heart and can be dangerous, so talk to your doctor first if you have any heart, blood pressure or circulation condition.

## What the evidence shows

| Question | Grade | Evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Muscle soreness after strenuous exercise | Moderate | [28 studies meta-analysed](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01800-1): cold water immersion superior to other methods for soreness; similar for power and flexibility; more effective than active recovery, contrast therapy and warm-water immersion for most outcomes; air cryotherapy better for strength | Active populations; short-term outcomes; the abstract does not state temperature or duration effects, which were rarely moderators |
| Longevity, metabolic or mood benefits | Not shown | None verified | No claim made |
