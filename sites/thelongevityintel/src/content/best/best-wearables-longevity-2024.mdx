**The short answer:** no wearable has been shown to lengthen life, so pick on cost and use. Over two years Oura Ring 5 costs about $539 including membership, WHOOP $398 to $718 depending on tier, and an Apple Watch Series 12 costs from $399 with no fee. A prescription-free glucose sensor costs about $89 a month (Stelo) or $54 for two weeks (Lingo). Independent studies found overnight heart rate and HRV the most trustworthy outputs; sleep stages and VO2 estimates the least. None of these diagnoses anything. Prices checked 29 Sept 2026.

## What independent studies found

| Question | Finding | Study and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Overnight HRV against ECG | Concordance 0.99 (Oura Gen 4), 0.97 (Oura Gen 3), 0.94 (WHOOP 4.0), 0.87 (Garmin Fenix 6) | [Physiol Rep 2025](https://doi.org/10.14814/phy2.70527): 13 adults, 536 nights; older generations than those sold now |
| Sleep against a sleep lab | Sleep versus wake about 86 to 89%; stages about 50 to 61% | [Sensors 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/s22166317): 53 adults; Oura Gen 2, WHOOP 3.0, Garmin Forerunner 245 |
| Apple Watch VO2 max | Under-read by 6.25 mL/kg/min, 13.2% error | [Mayo Clin Proc Digit Health 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mcpdig.2026.100357): 40 adults, Series 10 |
| Over-the-counter CGM | 12.9% mean absolute relative difference against capillary meters | [J Diabetes Sci Technol 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/19322968261482150): 39 young adults without diabetes |

The full metric-by-metric table, with more studies, is on [what the validation studies show](/wearables/wearable-accuracy-what-studies-show). We found no direct study of Oura Ring 5, WHOOP 5.0 and Apple Watch Series 12 against each other, so no ranking of current models on accuracy is possible.

## Choosing

- **You want the lowest ongoing cost and a screen:** an Apple Watch has no subscription and the strongest regulator-backed alerts (AFib, sleep apnea and hypertension notifications, per Apple and the FDA). It needs an iPhone.
- **You want to sleep and lift with one device on a ring:** Oura, with about $70 a year after the ring.
- **You want a screenless training and recovery coach:** WHOOP, understanding you rent the band: stop paying and it stops working. See [WHOOP vs Oura](/wearables/whoop-vs-oura-comparison) and [Garmin vs WHOOP](/wearables/garmin-vs-whoop-comparison).
- **You train outdoors and want GPS and days of battery:** Garmin; the Fenix 7 is superseded by the fenix 9, so check current prices.
- **You are curious about food and glucose:** a two-week Lingo or Stelo plan, not a standing subscription; the evidence for healthy adults is thin.
- **You have symptoms:** a doctor, not a readiness score.
