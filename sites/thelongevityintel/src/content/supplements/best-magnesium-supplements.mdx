**The short answer:** magnesium has moderate evidence for lowering blood pressure, and low-quality evidence that it shortens the time to fall asleep in older adults. The memory results for magnesium L-threonate come from one 30-day trial of a branded formula. If you have kidney disease or take certain medicines, magnesium can be unsafe to self-treat, so talk to your doctor first.

## What the evidence shows

Grades follow the [rubric](/database#rubric).

| Question | Grade | Evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blood pressure | Moderate | A [meta-analysis of 34 RCTs](https://doi.org/10.1161/HYPERTENSIONAHA.116.07664) (2,028 people): systolic -2.00 mm Hg (CI 0.43 to 3.58), diastolic -1.78 mm Hg; median dose 368 mg/day elemental magnesium for a median 3 months | Small change in a surrogate marker; residual heterogeneity per the authors; trials vary in form and population |
| Sleep onset in older adults | Weak | A [meta-analysis of 3 RCTs](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-021-03297-z) (151 people): sleep onset 17.36 minutes shorter (CI -27.27 to -7.44); total sleep time not significant | All trials at moderate-to-high risk of bias; evidence quality low to very low |
| Memory (L-threonate) | Emerging | [One RCT](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu14245235), 109 adults, Magtein PS 2 g/day for 30 days: better scores on all five memory subtests | Single 30-day trial of a branded formula containing phosphatidylserine and vitamins C and D, in healthy Chinese adults aged 18 to 65; the authors declare no competing interests |

Form matters for what you pay, not for proven outcome: no head-to-head trial in our search shows one form outperforming another.

## Safety

The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/others/) says adults aged 19 to 64 need 300 mg a day (men) or 270 mg (women) and that 400 mg or less a day from supplements is unlikely to cause harm; more can cause diarrhoea. Kidneys clear magnesium, so kidney disease raises the risk of accumulation. Magnesium can also interact with some antibiotics and other medicines; ask a pharmacist or your doctor before taking it. Diarrhoea is a common effect of higher supplement intakes. This page gives no personal dose.
