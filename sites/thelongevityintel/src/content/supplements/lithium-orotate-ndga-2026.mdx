**The short answer:** lithium orotate is interesting science, not yet a human-proven supplement. A 2025 Nature study found lithium was the only metal significantly lower in the brains of people with mild cognitive impairment, that dietary lithium depletion accelerated Alzheimer's-like changes in mice, and that lithium orotate prevented pathology and memory loss in mouse models. We found no human trial of lithium orotate supplements. Pharmaceutical lithium has a narrow therapeutic window. We could not verify claims about NDGA. Talk to your doctor.
