**The short answer:** curcumin's best-supported use is arthritis symptoms, and even that is cautious. A 2022 meta-analysis of 29 randomised trials (2,396 people, five types of arthritis) found curcumin or turmeric extract may improve inflammation and pain, but the authors warned the trials were low in quality and few. We found no trial showing a longevity benefit. Talk to your doctor; curcumin can interact with medicines such as blood thinners.

## What the evidence shows

Grade: Emerging (arthritis symptoms); not shown for longevity.
