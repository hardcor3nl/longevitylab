**The short answer:** the human evidence for gut microbiome supplements and healthy ageing is early. A 2026 systematic review of 14 studies (13 randomised trials) found probiotics, prebiotics and synbiotics improved global cognition scores in older adults in a subgroup meta-analysis (mean difference 1.28), but the authors note heterogeneity and a shortage of high-quality trials. Nothing we found shows longer life. Talk to your doctor before starting, especially if you are immunocompromised.

## What the evidence shows

Grade: Emerging (cognition in older adults); not shown for longevity.
