**The short answer:** ordinary fish oil has not been shown to prevent heart attacks or strokes in the big trials. VITAL (about 1 g/day) found no reduction in major cardiovascular events, STRENGTH (a high-dose product) found none, and only REDUCE-IT, using prescription purified EPA in high-risk patients already on statins, found a reduction. A meta-analysis links omega-3 supplements to more atrial fibrillation, more so above 1 g/day. Talk to your doctor before taking it, especially with heart disease or blood thinners.

## The four studies that matter

Grades follow the [rubric](/database#rubric); the overall grade for over-the-counter fish oil for preventing cardiovascular events is Weak (no benefit shown in VITAL, a null result in STRENGTH), and Moderate only for the prescription EPA product in one high-risk group.

| Study | Who and what | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [VITAL](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1811403) | 25,871 adults, 1 g/day marine n-3 (the [trial's capsule](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7089819/) held 840 mg EPA+DHA), median 5.3 years, NIH-funded | Major cardiovascular events HR 0.92 (CI 0.80 to 1.06); heart attack HR 0.72 (secondary); no excess bleeding |
| [REDUCE-IT](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1812792) | 8,179 patients with high triglycerides on statins, 4 g/day prescription icosapent ethyl, median 4.9 years, funded by the drug's maker (Amarin) | Primary endpoint 17.2% vs 22.0% (HR 0.75); more atrial fibrillation hospitalisation (3.1% vs 2.1%); serious bleeding 2.7% vs 2.1% |
| [STRENGTH](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2020.22258) | 13,078 high-risk statin-treated patients, 4 g/day omega-3 carboxylic acids vs corn oil | HR 0.99; stopped early for low probability of benefit; more gastrointestinal events (24.7% vs 14.7%) |
| [Meta-analysis of 7 RCTs](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.121.055654) | 81,210 patients | Atrial fibrillation HR 1.25; HR 1.49 above 1 g/day vs 1.12 at 1 g/day or less |

Reading across them: the benefit appears tied to a specific prescription product in a specific high-risk group, not to over-the-counter fish oil in general. Why REDUCE-IT differs from STRENGTH (dose, formulation, placebo oil) is debated; we do not claim to settle it.
