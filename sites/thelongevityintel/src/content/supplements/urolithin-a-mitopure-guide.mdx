**The short answer:** urolithin A has several small human trials, but the headline claims rest on secondary results and every trial we read is tied to Amazentis, which makes the Mitopure ingredient. In 66 older adults the primary endpoints (6-minute walk and muscle ATP) did not significantly improve; in middle-aged adults muscle strength rose about 12% but the primary endpoint, peak power, did not. At the 1,000 mg a day used in two trials it costs about $198 a month as Timeline's Mitopure bought once ($178 on monthly delivery), $138 as Omre and $90 as Mt. Angel (brand pages, 29 Sept 2026). Talk to your doctor before starting.

## What the trials show

Grades follow the [rubric](/database#rubric); overall grade: Emerging.

| Trial | Who and what | Primary endpoint | Other findings | Ties |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Liu 2022, JAMA Netw Open](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.44279) | 66 adults aged 65 to 90; 1,000 mg/day or placebo for 4 months | 6-minute walk distance and maximal muscle ATP: no significant improvement | Muscle endurance improved at 2 months (secondary); lower acylcarnitines, ceramides and C-reactive protein | Authors affiliated with Amazentis |
| [Singh 2022, Cell Rep Med](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35584623/) | Middle-aged adults; two doses for 4 months | Peak power output: not significantly improved | Muscle strength up about 12%; aerobic endurance and 6-minute walk improvements described as clinically meaningful; lower C-reactive protein | Amazentis sponsored; several authors employees or board members |
| [Denk 2025, Nat Aging](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41174221/) | 50 healthy middle-aged adults; 1,000 mg/day or placebo for 4 weeks | T cell changes: naive-like CD8+ cells up 0.50 percentage points (CI 0.16 to 0.83) and CD8+ fatty acid oxidation capacity | Immune markers, not illness or infection outcomes | Amazentis funded it and supplied Mitopure; employees among authors |

What the trials do not show: any effect on lifespan, disease risk or long-term safety. Nothing here compares urolithin A with exercise, which is the established way to build muscle and fitness.
