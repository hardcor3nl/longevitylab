**The short answer:** urolithin A has several small human trials, but the strongest headline claims rest on secondary results. In a 4-month trial in 66 older adults the primary endpoints (6-minute walk distance and muscle ATP production) did not significantly improve. In middle-aged adults muscle strength rose about 12% but the primary endpoint, peak power output, did not significantly improve. The trials we read were sponsored by or tied to the maker of Mitopure. It costs about $178 a month at the dose the JAMA trial used. Talk to your doctor before starting.

## What the trials show

Grades follow the [rubric](/database#rubric); overall grade: Emerging.

| Trial | Who and what | Primary endpoint | Other findings | Ties |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Liu 2022, JAMA Netw Open](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.44279) | 66 adults aged 65 to 90; 1,000 mg/day or placebo for 4 months | 6-minute walk distance and maximal muscle ATP: no significant improvement | Muscle endurance improved at 2 months (secondary); lower acylcarnitines, ceramides and C-reactive protein | Authors affiliated with Amazentis |
| [Singh 2022, Cell Rep Med](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100633) | Middle-aged adults; two doses for 4 months | Peak power output: not significantly improved | Muscle strength up about 12%; better aerobic endurance and 6-minute walk described as clinically meaningful | Amazentis sponsored; several authors employees or board members |
| [Denk 2025, Nat Aging](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-025-00996-x) | 50 healthy middle-aged adults; 1,000 mg/day or placebo for 4 weeks | Immune cell changes: expanded naive-like CD8+ cells (0.50 percentage points, CI 0.16 to 0.83) and CD8+ fatty acid oxidation capacity | Immune markers, not illness or infection outcomes | Co-authors include Singh, D.Amico and Rinsch, who are Amazentis authors on the other trials |

What the trials do not show: any effect on lifespan, disease risk or long-term safety. Nothing here compares urolithin A with exercise, which is the established way to build muscle and fitness.
