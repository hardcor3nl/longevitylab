**The short answer:** the human evidence for lion's mane is small. A 2025 systematic review found five randomised trials and three pilot trials; on the Mini-Mental State Examination one RCT and one pilot showed a combined weighted mean increase of 1.17 points in the treated group. Most of the mechanism work is in cells and animals. Nothing shows dementia prevention or longer life. Talk to your doctor before starting.
