**The short answer:** berberine lowers HbA1c and fasting glucose in people with type 2 diabetes: a meta-analysis of 46 randomised trials found mean reductions of 0.73 in HbA1c and 0.86 in fasting glucose. We found no trial showing benefit for longevity in people without diabetes. In one pilot, about a third of patients had transient gastrointestinal side effects. It can interact with medicines. Talk to your doctor before taking it, especially alongside diabetes drugs.
