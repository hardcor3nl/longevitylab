**The short answer:** alpha-lipoic acid (ALA) has evidence in one condition. A 2023 meta-analysis of 10 randomised trials (1,242 patients) found oral ALA improved total symptom score, neurological disability score and global satisfaction in diabetic polyneuropathy, but not pain scores, vibration perception or nerve conduction. No trial we found supports a longevity claim. ALA can lower blood sugar, so talk to your doctor, especially if you take diabetes medicines.

## What the evidence shows

Grade: Moderate for diabetic neuropathy symptoms; not shown for longevity.

| Question | Evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Diabetic polyneuropathy | [10 RCTs, 1,242 patients](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15163634): better total symptom score (dose-related trend), disability score and global satisfaction; no favourable result for VAS, vibration perception threshold, NIS-LL or nerve conduction | Patients with diabetic neuropathy; symptom scores are subjective |
| Longevity in healthy people | None found | No claim made |
