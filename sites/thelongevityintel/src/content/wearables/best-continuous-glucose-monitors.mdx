**The short answer:** If you do not have diabetes and want to try a continuous glucose monitor (CGM), you have two direct options in the US: Dexcom's Stelo, listed at $89 a month with 15-day sensors, and Abbott's Lingo, listed at $54 for a two-week plan that does not auto-renew (both checked 29 Sept 2026). Services such as Nutrisense and Levels sell coaching on top. Prescription models like Libre 3 and Dexcom G7 are made for people with diabetes. Treat the readings as patterns: an independent study of over-the-counter CGMs found 12.9% average error, and no trial shows a CGM improves health in healthy people.

## What the evidence shows

- **Accuracy.** A [September 2026 study](https://doi.org/10.1177/19322968261482150) of over-the-counter CGMs in 39 young adults without diabetes found 12.9% mean absolute relative difference against capillary meters, worse after a sugary drink (15.3% vs 10.6%). The authors say the sensors track overall trends but are less reliable for exact values.
- **Sensor life.** Stelo's own footnote says about 20% of sensors may not last the full 15 days. Dexcom's G7 page gives about 26% for that model. Expect to replace some sensors early.
- **Benefit.** A [2024 meta-analysis of 25 randomised trials](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-024-01692-6) found CGM feedback lowered HbA1c by 0.28% and raised time in range by 7.4 points, but most trials were in people with diabetes, only 3 were in obesity, and 44% reported CGM-company conflicts. We found no trial showing benefit in healthy adults.

## Who it suits, who should skip it

**Reasonable to try for a fortnight:** adults without diabetes who are curious how meals and sleep move their glucose and will change a habit if the graph surprises them. Start with the cheapest way in.

**Skip it, or ask a doctor first:** anyone with a history of disordered eating (Lingo itself advises consulting a professional), people on glucose-lowering medicines, and anyone who wants a diagnosis. A CGM does not diagnose diabetes. If you are worried, ask for a laboratory test; see [best at-home blood tests](/diagnostics/best-at-home-blood-tests-2024).
