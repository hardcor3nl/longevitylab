**The short answer:** over two years Oura Ring 5 costs about $539 (a $399 ring plus $69.99 a year), while WHOOP costs $398, $478 or $718 on yearly billing ($319, $399 or $599 on its 24-month plans, per a tracker), because its band comes with the membership. Pick WHOOP if you want a screenless training and recovery coach on a subscription; pick Oura if you want a ring and the lowest ongoing fee. In the one study that used an ECG as the reference, Oura's overnight HRV matched more closely than WHOOP 4.0's, though both are older hardware. Neither device diagnoses anything.

## Two-year cost, US

Our arithmetic from the prices below, before tax and any promotions. Checked 29 Sept 2026.

| | WHOOP One | WHOOP Peak | WHOOP Life (MG) | Oura Ring 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Up-front | included in membership | included | included | $399 (Silver, Brushed Silver, Black); $499 for Gold, Stealth, Deep Rose |
| Yearly | $199 | $239 | $359 | $69.99 |
| Two years, paying yearly | $398 | $478 | $718 | $538.98 |
| Two years, 24-month plan (tracker figure) | $319 | $399 | $599 | not offered |
| Device | WHOOP 5.0 | WHOOP 5.0 | WHOOP MG | Ring 5, sizes 6 to 13 |
| Battery (maker's claim) | 14+ days | 14+ days | 14+ days | 6 to 9 days |

Sources: WHOOP prices are from its [8 May 2025 launch release](https://insider.fitt.co/press-release/whoop-unveils-whoop-5-0-and-whoop-mg/) and a [pricing tracker updated 13 May 2026](https://trackervs.com/pricing/whoop-pricing/), which reports no US price change since launch and lists the 24-month plans ($319, $399, $599). WHOOP's own US membership and support pages returned errors to our reader, so we could not confirm the current US figures on whoop.com; an [archived copy of WHOOP's Canadian membership page](https://web.archive.org/web/20260914074956/https://www.whoop.com/ca/en/membership/) (14 Sept 2026) shows the same three tiers priced in Canadian dollars. Check the plan page before buying. The tracker says WHOOP's monthly billing is open only to existing members and still needs a 12-month commitment. Oura's $69.99 a year ($5.99 a month, first month free) is from [Oura's membership page](https://ouraring.com/membership), read 29 Sept 2026; the $399 ring price and the $499 finishes are from [TechCrunch's June 2026 review](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/04/oura-ring-5-review-thinner-lighter-better/) because Oura's store page showed only the EUR 429 price to our reader. The older Ring 4 is still supported but we could not find its current US price.

What you get differs by WHOOP tier, per the archived WHOOP membership page. One: WHOOP 5.0, sleep, strain and recovery scores, coaching, VO2 max and heart rate zones, women's hormonal insights. Peak adds Healthspan (WHOOP Age) and Pace of Aging, a Health Monitor with alerts and a real-time Stress Monitor, and comes with a wireless PowerPack. Life swaps the device for the WHOOP MG and adds daily blood pressure insights, a Heart Screener with ECG readings and irregular heart rhythm notifications. WHOOP labels the ECG and rhythm features as medically regulated and not available in every region, and blood pressure insights as not a medical device.

## What independent studies found

| Measure | Result | Study and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Overnight HRV vs ECG | Oura Gen 4 concordance 0.99 (5.96% error), Oura Gen 3 0.97 (7.15%), WHOOP 4.0 0.94 (8.17%) | [Physiological Reports 2025](https://doi.org/10.14814/phy2.70527), 13 healthy adults, 536 nights; older generations than Ring 5 and WHOOP 5.0 |
| Sleep vs wake, against sleep lab | WHOOP 3.0 and Oura Gen 2 both matched about 86 to 89% | [Sensors 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/s22166317), 53 adults |
| Sleep stages, against sleep lab | Oura Gen 2 61%; WHOOP 3.0 about 60% | Same study; the authors judged devices fine for timing and duration but needing improvement on stages |
| WHOOP heart rate and HRV vs ECG | Acceptable heart rate agreement; HRV needed cautious reading | [2021 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34065516/); a listed author's role became WHOOP-sponsored after data collection |
| Six wrist devices vs polysomnography, incl. WHOOP 4.0 | Stage agreement fair to moderate (kappa 0.21 to 0.53); specificity for wake 29 to 52% | [Sleep Advances 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40303381/), 62 adults, one night; Oura not included |

A [Gen 3 multi-night study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38382312/) of 96 adults reported good agreement on several global sleep measures; three of its authors declare financial support from Oura. The authors of the Sensors 2022 study belong to a research group that receives support from WHOOP, which says it was not involved in that study. We found no study that compares WHOOP 5.0 and Oura Ring 5 directly, so nobody can say which current model is more accurate. HRV and recovery scores are proprietary; compare trends on one device, not scores between brands.

## Which to choose

- **WHOOP** if you train most days and want strain, recovery and coaching in one loop, and you are content to rent the hardware: stop paying and the band stops working.
- **Oura** if you want to wear one thing in bed and while lifting, prefer a one-off ring purchase, and want the cheaper yearly fee.
- **Neither** for sleep disorders or heart symptoms. Snoring, breathing pauses or palpitations need a doctor, not a readiness score.
